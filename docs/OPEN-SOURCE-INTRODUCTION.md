# Introducing Embassy Commons on the existing DBE website

Ready English copy and placement proposal. The existing Dutch Bitcoin Embassy website has not been modified. The full Sites review environment is owner-private; the source repository remains public for independent reuse. A separate minimal introduction is built at `dist/public-introduction/`.

## Placement

1. **Community page:** immediately after the three circles and before the newsletter/footer. This connects the Dutch community story to independent initiatives abroad.
2. **Homepage:** a short teaser before the final community call to action.
3. **Footer:** a permanent **Embassy Commons** link.
4. **Dedicated English page:** `/commons/`, with overview, open-source explanation and optional introduction.

Publish only the separate introduction folder at this route. Never copy the full review `dist/` into a public landing page. The minimal build contains no toolkit JSON, source template, source ZIP or internal documents. It intentionally links to the public open-source repository. An application form or hidden navigation is not access control.

## Dedicated introduction

Title: **Open source. Independent by design.**

Introduction:

> A volunteer initiative to help people build independent Bitcoin communities. We share software, knowledge and experience so local teams can move themselves and Bitcoin forward.

Independence statement:

> Your team owns and runs its website, source repository, hosting, data, programme and decisions. No DBE account, central database, paid subscription, exclusivity, contribution back or ongoing DBE service is required to run the supplied toolkit.

Optional assistance:

> You can use the open-source toolkit without asking our permission. If guidance would help, tell us about your team and your plans. We can discuss a first step together; support depends on volunteer availability.

The original code is MIT. Original playbooks and substantive documentation are CC BY 4.0. Original generic demo templates use CC0. Fonts have their own open licences. DBE names, logos, photography and external source material have separate rights; a local initiative uses its own identity. Private member data is not included.

## Short introduction

Fields: name, email, country, city or region, team, plans and an optional HTTPS website/community link. No identity documents, financial records or member lists are requested.

The form prepares an email to **info@dutchbitcoinembassy.com**, verified in the live official footer on 11 October 2026. Recheck the address before integration. The person reviews the text and sends it from their own email app. There is no automatic email, server-side application store, receipt claim, invitation or access grant. Changing a field invalidates the prepared email until it is prepared again.

Button: **Prepare introduction**

Ready state: **Your introduction is ready. It has not been sent. Review the text, then open your email app or copy the message.**

Fallback: copy the message and email it manually. The person sends the actual application, rather than the website silently sending anything.

## Access boundaries

- Public open-source files may be reused and redistributed under their stated licences. Existing grants are not revoked by changing website or repository visibility.
- The current owner-private review environment protects its direct JSON, ZIP and source-template routes through hosting access control.
- The public introduction includes only its own assets and an optional contact flow. No unpublished data is bundled.
- New private information, optional collaboration and agreed brand use need their own controls and arrangements. They cannot be hidden securely with browser navigation alone.
- Shared-publisher review rules govern that publisher's releases. Independent teams determine their own processes.

No public integration into the existing website has been performed. The bundle is prepared for that next step after review.
